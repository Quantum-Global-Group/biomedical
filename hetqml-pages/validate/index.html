What this view answers — and what to question

Validate splits the model score into five independently-sourced axes and asks you to commit. The radar chart lets you see at a glance which axis is the weakest; the skeptic view writes the counter-argument; the decision panel logs your call with full provenance for audit.

✓Clinical (FDA approval), Mechanism (anchor target alignment), Model (PR-AUC), Baseline (Δ vs classical), Artifact (integrity guards)
✓Composite trust score = mean of 5 axes; failed axes shown in red on the radar
INTERROGATE
⊙Does any axis fall below the dashed 65% threshold? If so, the score alone is misleading
⊙Where does the trust come from? Five axes, each independently sourced
02Reliability Diagramcalibration · 10 bins
PROVIDES
✓Predicted probability vs observed positive rate, binned at 10 deciles
✓Brier, ECE, MCE, Log loss summary for the top model
INTERROGATE
⊙Is the curve close to the diagonal? Deviation upward = under-confident, downward = over-confident
⊙Is ECE < 0.05? Higher and predicted probabilities should not be reported as actual probabilities
